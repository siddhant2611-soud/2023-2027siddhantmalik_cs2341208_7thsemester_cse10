About the Internship
Glitz Events India (founded 2024) specializes in luxury weddings, destination events, corporate galas and thematic celebrations. During the internship I worked across the event cycle and contributed to most of the company's recent events, covering concept development, decor and styling direction, team and vendor coordination, and on-ground execution support.
